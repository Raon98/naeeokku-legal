FAQ

Are my photos uploaded?

No. Face detection and compositing happen only on your device. The original photo is not stored.

How do I move to a new device?

Before switching or deleting the app, use Settings → Backup → Export (turn on "Include Face Photos"), then Import on the new device.

I paid but didn't get coins.

Close the app completely and open it again; undelivered purchases are delivered on the next launch. If that doesn't help, email us with your Apple receipt.

How do I get a theme back?

Tap Restore Purchases in Settings or the Shop. Themes are restored; coins are not.

How do refunds work?

Request refunds from Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com. After a refund, only the remaining coins from that purchase are removed and your fish stay.

Why didn't my fish grow after feeding?

Growth meals count once every 2 hours per fish, up to 4 a day, and only when the fish is hungry (fullness under 80). The fish details screen shows when the next one counts.

Can I change a fish's photo?

No, the photo is fixed after adoption. You can rename a fish anytime.

What is Play?

Tap a fish and choose Play: it chases a light for a few seconds. Up to twice a day (an hour apart) it also helps the fish grow.

How do I share a tank video?

Tap the video button at the top. An 8-second clip is made on your device and the share sheet opens. Please get consent before sharing videos that show other people's faces.

Can fish die?

Only while the app is open and a fish stays at 0 fullness for 30 minutes, after a warning. Fish never die while the app is closed.
