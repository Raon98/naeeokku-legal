Naeokku Privacy Policy

Effective: 10 November 2026 · 한국어

Dandokhaengdong ("we") respects the privacy of everyone who uses "Naeokku - My Own Aquarium" ("the app").

1. Data the app processes

The app does not collect personal data or send it to us.

  • There is no sign-up, login or server.
  • Game data (fish, coins, decorations, memories, settings) is stored only on your device.
  • No advertising, analytics, location, contacts or advertising identifier (IDFA) is used, and you are not tracked.
  • We do not share, outsource or transfer personal data to anyone.

2. Photos and face images

  • Only the single photo you pick in the iOS photo picker is passed to the app. The app never asks for access to your whole library.
  • Face finding, background removal and compositing use Apple's on-device features and happen entirely on your device. Photos are never sent to us or any third party. Faces are not used to identify people or create biometric data.
  • The original photo is not stored. Only the cropped face is kept, protected inside the app and excluded from device backups (iCloud).
  • Please get permission before using someone else's photo.

3. Video sharing

Tank videos made with "Share" are created on your device and go only to the app you choose (such as Photos or Instagram). We never receive them; the chosen service's policies apply after sharing.

3-1. Notifications

Hunger reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device. Nothing is sent anywhere. You can turn them off in Settings.

4. In-app purchases

Payments are handled by Apple. We never receive your payment details. The app only checks on your device that a purchase completed and records its transaction ID. Apple's privacy policy applies.

5. If you email us

We use your email address, message and any attachment (such as a receipt) only to reply and to handle payment or refund issues. Records of complaints and disputes are kept for 3 years as required by Korean e-commerce law; otherwise they are deleted once resolved. Please don't send photos by email.

6. Retention and deletion

App data stays on your device. Deleting a memory also deletes face images no longer used. Settings > Backup > Reset Data, or deleting the app, removes all app data and face images. Backup files you export are under your control.

7. Children

The app collects no personal data from anyone, of any age.

8. Your rights

You can view, change or delete in-app data yourself at any time. For information we hold from an email, you can ask us to access, correct, delete or stop processing it.

9. This web page

These pages are hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub may process access logs under its own privacy policy. We use no cookies or analytics here.

11. Changes

We will announce changes in an app update or on this page at least 7 days before they take effect.
